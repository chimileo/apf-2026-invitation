The Office of Cabinet Affairs and Strategic Coordination (OCASC) requests the pleasure of your presence at the

Annual Planning Forum 2026

for Planning Officers

from Monday, 7 December to Friday, 11 December 2026
in Phuentsholing, Chhukha Dzongkhag

The Forum brings together Planning Officers from ministries, agencies, Dzongkhags and Thromdes to take stock of progress under the 13th Five Year Plan, prepare the annual plans for the coming fiscal year, and share what is working across the planning community.

With warm regards,Office of Cabinet Affairs and Strategic Coordination

Getting there

Venue

Phuentsholing is the gateway town on Bhutan's southern border, where the highway from Thimphu comes down from the hills to meet the plains.

December weather
